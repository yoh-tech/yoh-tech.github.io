Frequently asked questions

You can send up to 20 files, 500 MB in total, per transfer. Any file format can be sent — photos and videos, as well as PDFs and documents. Choose photos and videos with "Select from Photos & Videos", and other files with "Add Files", from the Files app, iCloud Drive and so on.

No. When the transfer is complete, a download URL is issued for each file. The recipient just opens the URL and can download the file in a web browser, without the app.

Files are kept on the server for 3 days after sending, and can be downloaded up to 10 times during that period. Files are deleted automatically when the storage period ends.

On the transfer-complete screen, use "Compose Email" to send it by email, or "Send Another Way" to send it with any app you use, such as LINE or Messages. You choose the recipient in the email or in each app.

Communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
