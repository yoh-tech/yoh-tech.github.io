LoudBatch

Loudness checker for CarDSPMusicPlayer (Android)

Measures the loudness of your entire music library on your Mac in one go, and writes a gain value for each song to a CSV file. Import that CSV into CarDSPMusicPlayer, and the time-consuming "Loudness normalization" analysis on your device is finished instantly.

Why

Let your Mac do the heavy analysis

Songs ripped from old CDs and recent, loudly mastered songs sound very different in level at the same volume setting. CarDSPMusicPlayer corrects this automatically, but it analyzes songs a little at a time so as not to disturb playback, so a large library takes a long time.

Analysis on the device only

Days to a week

For about 10,000 songs. A car head unit takes tens of seconds per song.

Analysis with LoudBatch (Mac)

Tens of minutes

Tested with a library of about 14,000 songs. Several songs are analyzed in parallel.

How to use

How to use

  1. Install

    Open the downloaded .dmg and drag LoudBatch into your Applications folder.

  2. Analyze your music folder

    Launch LoudBatch, choose the folder that contains your music files, and start the analysis. All subfolders are included.

  3. A CSV is created

    volume_gains.csv is created directly inside the folder you chose. If you stop partway, the results so far are kept.

  4. Import it on Android

    Copy the CSV to your Android device and import it into CarDSPMusicPlayer. Songs are matched by file name, and the gain values take effect immediately.

The LoudBatch app itself is currently in Japanese only.

gain_db is the gain for each song (target − measured, limited to ±12 dB). The length of silence at the start and end of each song is also recorded and used by "Trim silence between songs". A file that can't be read doesn't stop the whole run; only that row is marked error. The actual CSV also has columns for the relative path and the title.
